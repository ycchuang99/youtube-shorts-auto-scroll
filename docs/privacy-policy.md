# Privacy policy — YouTube Shorts Auto Scroll

Effective date: October 20, 2026

## English

YouTube Shorts Auto Scroll is an independent Chrome extension maintained by Evan Chuang. Its purpose is to automate scrolling and provide playback controls in the YouTube Shorts feed.

**Information handled on your device.** The extension reads the current YouTube page route, the visible Shorts card, video playback events, and page elements that identify sponsored cards. It uses this information in the browser to provide the features you enable. It does not record your watch history, collect video titles or viewing activity, or send page content to the developer.

**Preferences and Chrome sync.** The extension saves the auto-scroll setting, sponsored-card skip setting, and selected playback speed using `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome may synchronize these preferences through your Google account if browser sync is enabled. This transfer is handled by Chrome and is subject to Google's services and privacy settings. The developer does not operate a server to receive these preferences and does not have access to your synced settings.

**No analytics or advertising profiles.** The extension contains no analytics SDK, external telemetry, publisher-operated data collection endpoint, or third-party tracking code. It does not sell user data, build advertising profiles, or intercept YouTube network requests. All extension executable code is included in the installed package.

**Permissions.** Storage access saves preferences. Access to YouTube pages lets the content script detect when you enter or leave Shorts and operate the active Shorts card. The script is registered across YouTube because the site can navigate into Shorts without a full page reload; player actions remain restricted to Shorts. The extension does not request `activeTab` or `tabs` permission.

**Your choices.** You can change settings in the popup, manage browser sync through Chrome, or disable or uninstall the extension through Chrome's extension settings. The extension has no separate account or server-side user profile to delete.

**Contact and updates.** Please use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ycchuang99/youtube-shorts-auto-scroll/issues) for privacy questions. Do not include private information in public issue reports. Policy changes will be published here with an updated effective date.
